# DSA 8401 Applied Machine Learning — Course Outline

DSA 8401 is a graduate unit in Applied Machine Learning offered at Strathmore University.
The unit is assessed by continuous assessment (40%) and a final examination (60%).
Continuous assessment is made up of eight practical labs, each worth 5% of the final mark.

Labs are submitted as Jupyter notebooks. A submission must contain the code, the output of
every cell, and a short written interpretation of each result. A notebook whose cells have
not been executed scores zero, because the marker cannot verify the claimed results.

The unit runs over fourteen weeks. Weeks one to six cover classical supervised learning,
weeks seven to ten cover deep learning, and weeks eleven to fourteen cover applied topics
including retrieval-augmented generation and model deployment.

The required software stack is Python 3.12, scikit-learn, TensorFlow/Keras, pandas and
numpy. Students working on machines without a GPU are expected to use Google Colab for the
deep learning labs (Lab 06 onwards).

The late submission policy deducts 10% of the lab mark per day late, up to a maximum of
five days, after which the lab scores zero. Extensions require written approval from the
unit lecturer before the deadline, not after it.

Academic integrity: students may discuss approaches with each other but must write their
own code. Use of AI coding assistants is permitted provided the notebook includes a short
declaration naming the tool and describing what it was used for.
